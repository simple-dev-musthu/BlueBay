Island Experiences & Services

Complete, end-to-end island management. From hassle-free government e-permits and boutique stays to thrilling lagoon water sports and private island boat excursions.

WHAT WE PROVIDE

Comprehensive Island Services

Everything handled by our on-ground team on Agatti Island.

Govt Entry Permits & PCC Assistance

Mandatory e-permit processing for all Indian travelers. We verify your Aadhaar, passport photos, and Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) and secure authorized entry permits directly.

Curated Island Tour Packages

Complete itineraries including airport AC transfers, accommodation, all daily meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Tea), inter-island boat trips, and personal island assistance.

View Tour Packages →

Accommodation & Resort Booking

From authentic cultural village homestays and beachfront cottages to luxury beach resorts and premium handcrafted wooden huts with private ocean sundecks.

Visiting Kalpitti & Bangaram Island

Embark on scenic mechanized boat safaris to the untouched atoll of Kalpitti Island for turtle watching and pristine reef walks, alongside day excursions to Bangaram Island—famed for its vast turquoise lagoon, sandbanks, and tranquil tropical beauty.

Complimentary Lagoon Water Sports

Included in all our packages: guided coral reef snorkeling, calm lagoon kayaking, and scenic glass-bottom boating with all life vests and safety gear provided.

Sunrise Dolphin Watching Excursions

Set out at dawn on private mechanized boats with experienced local boatmen to watch wild dolphins leaping joyfully in the calm morning lagoon waters.

PADI Scuba Diving (Optional Add-on)

Experience the underwater wonderland of Lakshadweep with certified PADI dive instructors. Discover scuba diving sessions suitable for beginners and non-swimmers!

Jet Ski & Parasailing Adventures

High-speed lagoon jet ski rides and thrilling parasailing sessions providing a bird's eye view over the coral atolls (subject to wind and sea weather conditions).

Deep Sea Fishing & Island Folklore

Offshore big-game fishing charters, night fish hunting, and evening traditional island folklore music and dance performances celebrating authentic Lakshadweep culture.
