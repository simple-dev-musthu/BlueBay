Curated Stay Packages

Select from welcoming local homestays to exclusive beachfront resorts and signature wooden huts.

Homestay

1. Standard Stay

₹13,500/- per person

A comfortable, budget-friendly stay in a welcoming local home, featuring a private room and a homely atmosphere. Ideal for travellers seeking an authentic island experience at an affordable price.

Itinerary
Day 1
Arrival & Agatti Island Discovery

Arrival at Agatti Island Airport, followed by a comfortable AC private transfer to your hotel. Enjoy a welcome drink, smooth check-in, and spend the day exploring the island, local culture, and scenic surroundings with lunch included.

Day 2
Kalpitti Island & Water Adventures

After breakfast, travel by mechanized boat to the untouched Kalpitti Island (30 minutes). Discover coral reefs and enjoy turtle watching before returning to Agatti for lunch. The afternoon includes kayaking, glass-bottom boating, and snorkeling.

Day 3
Dolphin Watch & Lagoon Experience

Set out after breakfast on a private mechanized boat for dolphin watching. Enjoy swimming, lagoon fishing, lunch, and snorkeling before returning to Agatti Island by 5:00 PM.

Day 4
Departure

After breakfast, proceed to the airport for your return journey, carrying unforgettable memories of Lakshadweep’s pristine beauty.

Note: The itinerary may be adjusted due to weather or sea conditions.
Inclusions
  • Entry permit & heritage fee
  • Airport transfers
  • Welcome drink
  • Accommodation
  • All meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Tea)
  • Sightseeing & inter-island boat transfers
  • Personal assistance
  • Glass bottom boating, snorkeling & kayaking
  • Kalpitti Island visit
Exclusions
  • Flight tickets
  • Scuba diving
Guidelines

Documentation & Permits: To process your mandatory Lakshadweep entry permit, each traveler must provide a clear copy of their Aadhaar Card and a recent passport-sized photograph. A verified Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) is strictly mandatory for all guests aged 18 years and above.

Payment Terms: A 30% advance payment is required upon confirmation of your flight tickets to confirm accommodation, inter-island boat clearance, and official entry permits.

Package Inclusions & Exclusions

Every Bluebay package is fully transparent so you know exactly what is included.

Package Inclusions

Included in all 5 accommodation packages:

  • Permits & transfers: Entry permit, heritage fee, and airport transfers.
  • Stay & dining: Accommodation and all meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner, and tea.
  • Sightseeing & island transfers: Sightseeing activities and inter-island boat transfers, as per the itinerary.
  • Water activities: Glass-bottom boating, snorkelling, kayaking, and a Kalpitti Island visit, subject to operational and weather conditions.
  • Personal assistance: Assistance throughout the trip for a smoother travel experience.

Package Exclusions

Not included in base package pricing:

  • Flight tickets: Airfare to and from the destination is not included.
  • Scuba diving: Available at an additional cost, subject to availability and applicable conditions.
Have Questions About Exclusions or Add-ons?

Our island coordinators can arrange scuba diving slots and guide you on flight schedules from Kochi (COK) to Agatti (AGX).

Important Travel Note

The itinerary may be adjusted depending on weather conditions, sea conditions, boat schedules, and local operational restrictions. Water activities and island visits are subject to availability and safety clearances.
