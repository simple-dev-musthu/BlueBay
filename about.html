OUR ISLAND ROOTS

Authentic Hospitality From People Who Know the Islands Best

At Bluebay Holidays, we believe travel to Lakshadweep should be as calm and effortless as the gentle turquoise tide. Navigating entry permits, flight connections, and inter-island boat logistics can feel daunting, but that is where our native ground team steps in.

Based right on Agatti Island, we personally oversee your airport transfers, government paperwork, speed boat excursions to uninhabited Kalpitti Island, and authentic home-cooked coastal meals. Every guest is treated like family.
