3 Nights / 4 Days Curated Itinerary

Complete day-by-day plan managed by Bluebay Holidays on the ground.

Day 1 – Arrival & Agatti Island Discovery

Arrival at Agatti Island Airport (AGX). AC private transfer to stay, welcome tender coconut drink, smooth check-in. Fresh island lunch included, followed by sightseeing: Eastern Jetty, local island village & famous Sunset Point. Dinner & overnight stay.

Day 2 – Kalpitti Island & Water Adventures

30-minute mechanized boat transfer to untouched, uninhabited Kalpitti Island. Coral reef exploration and sea turtle watching. Return to Agatti for lunch. Afternoon complimentary glass-bottom boating, kayaking & snorkeling session. Dinner & overnight stay.

Day 3 – Dolphin Watch & Lagoon Experience

Early morning private boat ride for dolphin watching in the open lagoon. Breakfast, lagoon swimming, traditional lagoon fishing, and leisure beach relaxation. Snorkeling & evening tea. Dinner & overnight stay.

Day 4 – Departure with Unforgettable Memories

Island breakfast, luggage assistance, and private AC airport transfer back to Agatti Airport for flight departure.
